Harsh Vandana Sharma

Founder & CEO of Sheryians Coding School

Harsh Vandana Sharma is the Founder and CEO of Sheryians Coding School, one of India's leading practical coding education platforms. Through his unique teaching style and project-based learning approach, he has helped thousands of students build real-world technical skills.

His vision is to make quality education accessible, practical, and industry-focused for every student. Instead of focusing only on theory, he encourages students to learn by building projects and solving real-world problems.

About Sheryians Coding School

Sheryians Coding School is the primary educational platform founded by Harsh Sharma. It provides practical coding education, mentorship, project-based learning, and career-focused guidance to students who want to build skills in technology and software development.

About Not Your College

Not Your College is a learning initiative created by the Sheryians team. Its goal is to provide high-quality educational content completely free on YouTube so that students can learn valuable skills without financial barriers. The platform focuses on structured playlists, practical learning, and skill development.
